# Data protection complaints process

**Status: Draft — requires legal review (H-23). Not legal advice.** Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; source: COMPLIANCE §2.5. Last updated 10 Nov 2026.

The 2025 Act requires a controller to give people a way to complain about how their personal data is used, to acknowledge a complaint within 30 days, and to respond without undue delay. The solicitor confirms the commencement date and wording.

## 1. How to complain

| Route | Detail |
|---|---|
| In the app | Me → Privacy and data → "Make a complaint about my data": a short form (what happened, what outcome the person wants) |
| By email | [privacy@domain], shown in the Privacy Policy and the app |
| Through a shop | The shop passes it to the platform within 2 working days; if the complaint is about the shop's own use of data, the platform tells the person the shop is responsible and helps them reach it |

No account is needed to complain by email.

## 2. What happens

| Step | Deadline | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Log the complaint: date received, who, summary | Same working day | Support administrator |
| Acknowledge in writing, with a reference and what happens next | Within **30 days** of receipt; target 5 working days | Support administrator |
| Investigate: take appropriate steps, make enquiries, keep the person informed | Without undue delay | Named data protection contact |
| Tell the person the outcome and the reasons, and that they can complain to the ICO | Without undue delay; target 30 days from receipt | Named data protection contact |
| Record the outcome and any fix | When closed | Named data protection contact |

If the complaint is also a rights request (for example "delete my data"), it is handled under [DSAR_AND_ERASURE.md](DSAR_AND_ERASURE.md) with its one-month deadline, and the complaint is answered alongside it.

## 3. If the complaint reveals a breach

The incident process in the RUNBOOK applies: contain, assess, and where required notify the ICO within 72 hours and tell the people affected.

## 4. If the person is not satisfied

The outcome letter tells them they can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, and how.

## 5. What the Privacy Policy must say

- How to complain to us (the routes above).
- That we acknowledge within 30 days and reply without undue delay.
- That the person can then go to the ICO.

## 6. Records

Complaint, correspondence, outcome and dates, kept 3 years after closure (proposed). The count of complaints is reviewed at each phase close until launch and monthly afterwards.
