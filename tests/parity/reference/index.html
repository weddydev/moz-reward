How it stays fast

  • Compositor first. Most motion is transform and opacity. Paint-heavy effects (liquid filter, orbiting border, text shine) stay on small elements.
  • One particle canvas for the whole app. It runs only while particles are alive, has a hard cap, and caps pixel density at 2×.
  • Device tiers. Phones with 4 or fewer cores, 4 GB or less memory, or Data Saver get Lite: half the particles, no liquid filter, no glass blur. This page shows the tier it picked at the top.
  • Loops sleep off-screen and when the tab is hidden. Signature moments load only on the screens that use them.
  • Proof before merge: 60 fps traces on a mid-range Android, and the existing Lighthouse and bundle budgets.

Safe and accessible

  • Reduce motion (system setting or the in-app switch) shows end states with short fades and no particles.
  • No strobing. Only one flash per moment, well under the WCAG limit of three flashes a second.
  • Never blocks. Every control works mid-animation; the intro skips on tap; data loads while it plays.
  • Review rules untouched. Every star rating shows the same two choices.
  • Text on gold stays ink; gold text uses deep gold for contrast.

Libraries

  • Keep Motion (already in the stack) for springs, layout and shared-element morphs.
  • Add nothing heavy. The particle engine on this page is a few KB of plain canvas code; it becomes src/components/motion/fx.
  • Optional: Rive, if a motion designer authors the logo intro and ingot as interactive files. Loaded on those screens only.
  • Skipped: GSAP (free now, but it would duplicate Motion), Lottie (heavier and not interactive).

Rollout without stopping the other developer

  1. Client sign-off on this concept, all of it or chosen rows.
  2. DESIGN.md v2 plus a DECISIONS entry: three motion tiers (quiet under 300 ms, expressive 300 to 700 ms, signature 1.2 to 2.6 s), a higher gold budget, the new components.
  3. A design sub-phase slotted in as new tasks: tokens and living background → particle engine and controls → member card, wallet stack, dock → Gilded Gate 2.0 and vault moments → restyle the already-built Wallet (P1-07) and Gold Vault (P1-08). Rough size: 6 to 8 working days.
  4. Most of the work lands in shared components (src/components/ui, src/components/motion), so screens still being built pick it up automatically.
Needs a decision: this goes against four current rules in DESIGN.md: "Premium calm" and "two or three wow moments" (§1), gold at no more than 10% of a screen (§3), and everyday motion under 300 ms with transform and opacity only (§7). If the client approves, those rules get rewritten, not quietly broken.
